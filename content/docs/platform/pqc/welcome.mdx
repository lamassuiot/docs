Lamassu negotiates post-quantum key exchange and authentication with devices, and issues post-quantum and composite certificates. This page lets you try it: combine the key exchange and authentication families in the configurator, explore the architecture and the algorithms, and see how independent clients fared in the interoperability tests. The reference documentation starts at the [overview](/docs/platform/pqc/overview).
