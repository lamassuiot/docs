Just want to evaluate the platform?

Use Fastlane to bring up PostgreSQL, Keycloak, RabbitMQ and Lamassu on a Kubernetes cluster through a single flow. It is intended for labs, demos and bootstrap; for production, configure dependencies and Helm values explicitly.
