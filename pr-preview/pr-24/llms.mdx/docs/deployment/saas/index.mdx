# Use Lamassu as SaaS (/docs/pr-preview/pr-24/deployment/saas)



Lamassu SaaS offers the platform's capabilities in a managed environment. It is the right option when your team wants to focus on PKI and device identities, not on maintaining the infrastructure that supports them.

What the service manages [#what-the-service-manages]

* Platform availability and maintenance.
* Automatic backups.
* Version upgrades.
* Technical support and SLA according to your plan.

Plans [#plans]

| Plan         |      Devices | Support        |     SLA |
| ------------ | -----------: | -------------- | ------: |
| Starter      |  Up to 1,000 | Email          |  99.5 % |
| Professional | Up to 10,000 | Email and chat |  99.9 % |
| Enterprise   |    Unlimited | Dedicated      | 99.99 % |

Request access [#request-access]

Contact the Lamassu team through [lamassu.io](https://lamassu.io) to request a demo or choose a plan.

When you receive your instance's credentials, continue with [Get started](/docs/platform/pki/quickstarts/overview).
