Use Lamassu as SaaS

Consume Lamassu as a managed service without operating your own infrastructure.

Sin cambios en esta página

Lamassu SaaS offers the platform's capabilities in a managed environment. It is the right option when your team wants to focus on PKI and device identities, not on maintaining the infrastructure that supports them.

What the service manages

  • Platform availability and maintenance.
  • Automatic backups.
  • Version upgrades.
  • Technical support and SLA according to your plan.
