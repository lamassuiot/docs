Renewal and recovery

Renew without losing the identity and recover devices according to their actual state.

Sin cambios en esta página

Renewal retains the device identifier and requests a successor certificate. The client must complete the process: check the response, install the matching pair and demonstrate that the consumer accepts it. Lamassu alerts notify you; they do not perform those steps.

Prepare renewal

  • Query the current certificate in Lamassu and compare it with the installed certificate. You need its subject, serial and dates, as well as the client key.
  • Check the re-enrollment authentication mode: it is independent of initial authentication. With CLIENT_CERTIFICATE, use the current identity; do not assume the bootstrap credential is admitted.
  • Calculate the window's start: the current certificate's NotAfter minus reenrollment_delta. Preventive and critical thresholds produce states/notifications; they do not extend that window.
  • Review Revoke on Re-enrollment and recovery if installation fails. When enabled, Lamassu can revoke the previous identity before the client installs the new one.

Request the successor certificate

The example continues the EST tutorial, in the same Bash session with set -euo pipefail, umask 077 and EST_BASE. Use a new key compatible with the profile. Conversion from the certificate preserves the subject and copies only SAN when present; check the subject and requested names before sending it. The backend checks the subject against the current identity: it attempts RawSubject equality and then compares CN, OU, O, L, ST and C. Rebuilding only the CN can lose required attributes.

The request authenticates with device.crt and device.key; the CSR contains the new public key. Also check extensions, dates and correspondence of the serial with the current slot in Lamassu. Do not resubmit a failed request without querying that state: issuance, previous revocation and successor binding can have partial results.

Install and check the new pair

Keep the result as device.next.crt and device.next.key until verified. Switch the pair through a coordinated operation in the device store or configuration; avoid temporarily exposing a certificate with another version's key.

Test mTLS with the consumer and verify that it uses the new serial. Confirm the history and current certificate in Lamassu. If Revoke on Re-enrollment was disabled, the previous identity can remain valid: retire it according to PKI policy after testing the successor, and check revocation publication and consumption.

If the previous identity is already revoked, retaining its files does not restore a valid connection. Recovery requires an admitted credential and authorized intervention according to the situation.

Recover according to state

SituationProcedure
Valid certificate, outside the windowSchedule the request within the window. Do not use simpleenroll to bypass it.
Expired certificate, key availableWith certificate authentication, review enable_expired_renewal and whether Gateway permits presenting that credential. Revocation, subject and the remaining checks still apply.
Renewal through webhook or NO_AUTHThe mTLS expiry check does not run in these modes. Define expiry and authorization policy in the corresponding admission process; the flag is not a universal prohibition.
Lost or inaccessible keyYou cannot prove possession of the previous identity. Authorize new admission and, if appropriate, controlled replacement; revoke the compromised identity.
Current identity revokedsimplereenroll rejects the revoked current certificate. Investigate the reason and prepare an authorized readmission procedure; do not disable authentication to force it.
Device without an identityUse initial enrollment. For an existing entry, check the replacement restriction in provisioning.
Retired deviceReview the operational decision before readmission. Decommissioned state and revocation of the entire history are not equivalent.
Timeout, HTTP 500 or installation failureInspect the device, issued certificates and history before retrying. A successor may have been issued or the previous identity revoked.

Continuous operation

Schedule renewal with time for disconnections, trust distribution and consumer checks. Stagger requests across the population and bound retries within the window; authentication, subject and policy errors require correcting their cause. Maintain an admitted and tested recovery mechanism for lost keys and pairs that could not be installed.
